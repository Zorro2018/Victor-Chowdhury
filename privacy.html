Privacy Policy — victorbuildsai.com

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This is the personal portfolio site of Victor Chowdhury. It includes "Talk to Victor", an AI chat that answers questions about Victor's work and lets visitors request a call. This page explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information visitors provide

  • Chat messages you send to the AI are stored so Victor can read them and follow up.
  • Contact and booking details you choose to share (name, email, company, role, a note, and a requested meeting time) are stored so Victor can respond and send a calendar invite.
  • Basic visit data (approximate location derived from IP address, and referrer) is recorded for site analytics and abuse prevention.

Chat messages are processed by Google's Gemini API to generate responses. Confirmation emails are sent through Resend. This information is never sold or shared for advertising.

Google Calendar access

The site's Google OAuth app is used only by Victor, for his own Google account. It requests access to his calendar so the chat can:

  • read free/busy times on his calendars to show open meeting slots (event titles and details are not read or shown to visitors), and
  • create, update, or delete events that it made itself for call requests from the chat, including inviting the requester and adding a Google Meet link once Victor approves.

The app does not access any visitor's Google account. Google user data is used only to provide this scheduling feature, isn't shared with third parties, and isn't used for advertising or to train AI models. Its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Retention and deletion

Chat logs and contact or booking details are kept only as long as needed to follow up. To have your information deleted, email victorchowdhury1988@gmail.com.
